# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate professionally, critique work rather than people, document claims
with evidence, respect privacy, and make the project accessible to people with different levels
of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report
conduct or privacy incidents privately through the repository's Security tab. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, reject contributions, or escalate abuse to GitHub.

Enforcement decisions will consider impact, intent, recurrence, remediation, and community
safety. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
